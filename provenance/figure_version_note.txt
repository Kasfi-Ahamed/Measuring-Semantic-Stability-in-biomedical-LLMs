  WHY THE FIRST TRACE WAS WRONG, because the method matters more than the correction.
  The trace matched figure basenames against script text. Eight figures are written from
  f-strings built out of a corpus slug, so their full basename never appears literally in the
  producing script and the search could not see it. The one script that DID contain every
  basename literally was the copier, which lists them to copy them. So the search returned a
  complete-looking answer that named the wrong layer for 23 of 26 figures, and the two plates
  with no literal match anywhere were reported as having no producer at all.

  Re-running the trace by STEM rather than full basename found producers for 20 of the 26 that
  the exact-name pass had missed. A name-based trace cannot see a templated output path, and a
  trace that returns one confident answer per figure is not evidence that it found the right
  one.

DID ANY PLOTTED VALUE MOVE? NO. THIS IS PRESENTATION ONLY.
  This was checked at COLUMN level against the data each figure actually reads, not inferred
  from the commit messages.

  The four risk-coverage figures read, per scripts/fig_report_risk_coverage.py CFG:
      cadec        outputs/rq3/umls_candidate_margin_cadec.csv          tracked, CLEAN
      medmentions  outputs/rq1/umls_candidate_margin_medmentions.csv    unchanged on disk
      qa           outputs/qa/qa_results_combined_identity_filtered.csv tracked, CLEAN
  None of their inputs is modified, so no plotted value in those four can have moved.

  fig_entropy_across_corpora reads outputs/rq3/entropy_cadec.csv, which IS modified. Its
  concept() reads exactly three columns:
      usecols=["model_name", "normalised_entropy_dedup", "retained_m_distinct"]
  Comparing the committed and working-tree versions of entropy_cadec.csv at zero tolerance
  with float_precision="round_trip":
      normalised_entropy_dedup   0 differing
      retained_m_distinct        0 differing
      model_name                 0 differing
  So every value this figure plots is byte-identical. The restyle is presentation only.

  WHAT DID MOVE IN entropy_cadec.csv, recorded because it is real even though unplotted:
      mapping_confidence   10,324 of 41,288 cells, max |delta| 4.178e-04, median 5.960e-08.
                           NOT floating-point noise: 0 of the 10,324 are within 4 ULP of the
                           value, and the median delta is exactly float32 epsilon near 1.0
                           (2^-24 = 5.96e-08), which points at a float32/float64 change in how
                           the column was produced rather than at a rounding artefact.
      dominant_cui         1 cell: cadec_LIPITOR.60_TT2 / Llama3-OpenBioLLM-8B, C2142181 ->
                           C5442019, with normalised_entropy identical at 0.5644754678724235.
                           An entropy-preserving flip, i.e. a tie between two equal-sized
                           clusters broken the other way.
      dominant_cui_dedup   the same 1 cell.
  Every entropy and accuracy column is unchanged. mapping_confidence IS a plotted signal in
  the risk-coverage figures, but those read it from umls_candidate_margin_*.csv, not from
  entropy_cadec.csv, and those files are clean.

  A SEPARATE PRE-EXISTING INCONSISTENCY, found while checking the above and not caused by the
  restyle: umls_candidate_margin_cadec.csv and entropy_cadec.csv disagree on
  mapping_confidence for 15,724 of 37,696 joined rows against the committed entropy file and
  21,437 against the working-tree one. The two artefacts were produced at different times and
  neither is a copy of the other. No figure reads both, so nothing reported is affected, but
  the two files are not interchangeable as a source for that column.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR ANYONE CHECKING THE MANUSCRIPT
  Comparing a figure in the submitted PDF against a fresh clone of v1.0.2 will show visible
  differences in these five plates -- tick grids, frame sizes, colours, and a legend label.
  Those differences are styling. The numbers behind them are identical. Use
  fix/figure-regenerations, not the tag, to reproduce the submitted plates.
