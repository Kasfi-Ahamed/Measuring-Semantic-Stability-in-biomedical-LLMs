WHY
  The 26-block MedMentions re-run needs roughly 2.05 GiB for new artefacts. The quota ceiling
  was calibrated empirically on 2026-10-10 -- a file grown in $HOME until EDQUOT, at
  200,144,601,746 bytes = 186.40 GiB = 200 GB decimal -- against 183.90 GiB used, leaving
  2.50 GiB. The previous disk guard reported 20.02 GiB of headroom at that moment because it
  read the quota as 200 GiB rather than 200 GB and added a hardcoded 21 GiB allowance for
  "home excluding data and outputs" that had drifted to 24.91 GiB. Both are fixed in
  scripts/disk_guard.py, which now measures used space with du and fails closed.

WHAT WAS KEPT AND WHY
  rq1_all_model_outputs_PRE_A9REMAP.csv stays on disk and is also archived gzipped. It was
  first believed to be the only surviving source of output_text for blocks 6-18. That belief
  was wrong: A9_CUTOFF carries output_text for blocks 0-19, and a 2,388-row sample from
  PRE_A9REMAP was found in A9_CUTOFF with byte-equal output_text in every case. It is
  redundant. It is retained as the provenance record of the re-map's input, not as insurance.

  A CONSEQUENCE THAT REACHES FURTHER. Because the mapped file carries output_text, the loss
  caused by scripts/mm_prune_mapped_shards.py is narrower than docs/STALE_ARTEFACTS.md
  deferred item 4 currently states. What was destroyed for blocks 0-5 and 19 is the per-shard
  partitioning of the generations, not the generated text itself. That deferred item, and any
  data availability statement drawn from it, needs correcting on those terms.

ROW-COUNT CAVEAT ADDED 2026-10-10, AFTER THE FACT
================================================================================
  Two row counts above came from wc -l on a file that no longer exists, so they cannot be
  re-derived and may be overstated:

    rq1_all_outputs_mapped.csv.bak.20260910T150307      758,560 physical lines
    rq1_all_outputs_mapped_CONTAMINATED_PRECUTOFF.csv 2,927,685 physical lines

  wc -l counts physical lines, and a CSV whose free-text fields contain newlines reads far
  higher than its record count -- rq3_cadec_validated_perturbations_full.csv reads 521,705 and
  holds 55,976, a 9.3-fold overcount. Both files above carry an output_text column, so the
  hazard applies to them in principle.

  WHAT STANDS AS THE EXACT RECORD for those two files: the SHA-256 and the byte size. Those
  were measured directly and are not affected.

  The other counts in this note are sound. The three A9 parts and A9_CUTOFF were corroborated
  against the pipeline's own amendment-9 receipts, which record len(df_mapped) and are record
  counts by construction; 2,572,698 + 2,202,290 + 2,557,257 = 7,332,245, matching A9_CUTOFF
  exactly. PRE_A9REMAP's 4,774,988 equals partA1 + partA2 from the same receipts. The CADEC
  and MedMentions margin and mapped-output counts were re-derived with pandas on 2026-10-10
  and are unchanged.

  See provenance/reading_hazards_note.txt for the general rule.
