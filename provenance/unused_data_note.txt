WHY IT WAS DELETED RATHER THAN ARCHIVED
  The directory raised no integrity question, only a data-holding one. Credentialed
  PhysioNet material whose approval for the intended use was still pending should not
  be copied to institutional storage, where it would persist for years unused and
  unaudited. The decision was Kasfi Ahamed's, taken 2026-10-05, scoped to this one
  directory. Nothing else was deleted.

  Recovering it, if anyone ever needs to, means a fresh credentialed download from
  physionet.org; the SHA256SUMS.txt shipped with the release lets a new copy be
  checked against what was held here, and the tree digest above lets the whole tree
  be checked.
